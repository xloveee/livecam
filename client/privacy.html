Privacy Policy — livecam Mobile

Last updated: May 2026

This policy describes how the livecam Mobile app (iOS and Android) handles information. The app is a client for self-hosted livecam servers (for example indep.stream).

What the app does

  • Connects to servers you configure (base URL, stream key, optional broadcast password).
  • Uses the camera and microphone when you choose to broadcast live video (WHIP/WebRTC).
  • Receives live video when you watch a room (WHEP or HLS).
  • Joins real-time chat via WebSocket to the same server.

Data stored on your device

  • Server profiles (name, URL, stream key, broadcast password) — stored locally; auth tokens in secure storage.
  • Chat nickname — stored locally.

Stream keys and passwords are not sent to us as the app developer; they are sent only to the servers you configure.

Data sent to your configured server

When you broadcast, watch, or chat, audio/video and chat messages are transmitted to your chosen livecam instance using WebRTC, HTTP, and WebSocket. That operator’s policies apply to data on their server.

Data we collect as app developer

We do not operate a central account service for the app. We do not receive your streams or chat unless you use our hosted demo server and that server’s separate policies apply.

App stores (Apple/Google) may collect standard download and crash diagnostics per their policies.

Permissions

  • Camera / Microphone — required for live broadcast.
  • Internet — required to reach your server.
  • Notifications (Android) — foreground service while broadcasting.

User-generated content

Live video and chat are user-generated. Server operators can moderate chat (ban, timeout, slow mode). Report abuse to the operator of the server you use.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Live streaming and chat are intended for adults or supervised use per store age ratings.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
