## Full description

livecam Mobile connects to your own livecam or indep.stream server. Broadcast live from your phone, watch streams with low latency, and join real-time chat — no Twitch or YouTube required.

Features:
• Add any livecam server (WHIP/WHEP)
• Go live with front/back camera
• Watch streams (WebRTC or HLS on iOS)
• Real-time chat with your audience
• Secure token login for broadcasters

You control your server, stream key, and audience. Requires a livecam-compatible server (self-hosted or hosted).
