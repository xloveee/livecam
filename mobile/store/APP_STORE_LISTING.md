## Description

livecam Mobile is the official companion app for the livecam self-hosted streaming platform.

Broadcast live video from your iPhone or iPad to your configured server using WHIP (WebRTC). Watch streams with low-latency WHEP or reliable HLS. Join room chat in real time.

• Configure server URL and stream key
• Bearer-authenticated broadcast login
• Front/back camera while live
• VP8-friendly encoding for cross-device viewers
• Background audio continuity during broadcast

Requires access to a livecam-compatible HTTPS server (e.g. indep.stream or your own deployment).
